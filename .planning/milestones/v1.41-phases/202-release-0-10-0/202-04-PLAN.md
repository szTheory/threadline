<objective>
Close the two gaps that let the publish pipeline look green while proving less than it claims,
and write down what to do when a publish goes wrong before one can.

Purpose: today a contract test asserts that the release workflow mentions the production
environment, and nothing asserts that the environment still has a required reviewer on GitHub's
side — deleting the reviewer leaves the gate green and inert. That is the third vacuous-gate
shape found in this repository within a week. Separately, the attestation row is written as soon
as the publish job reports success, before anything has proven the tarball installs. D-18, D-09
and D-10 fix both. D-17 and D-19 make the human gate and the recovery path honest in writing.

Output: a fail-closed live-state verifier and its own workflow, a post-publish smoke job wired
ahead of the attestation, strengthened control-plane assertions, and a recovery runbook.
</objective>
