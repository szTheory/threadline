# v1.17 Milestone Audit: Operator Surface Foundation

**Date:** 2026-05-06
**Status:** 🟢 PASSED

## Executive Summary
The v1.17 milestone successfully shipped a host-usable operator surface, providing an in-tree LiveView UI that turns v1.16 investigation contracts into one-click answers while preserving the v1.15 host-owns-auth boundary. The milestone demonstrated extreme architectural discipline by keeping Phoenix and LiveView as strictly optional dependencies.

## Requirement Verification

### SURF — Mountable in-tree surface and dependency posture
- ✅ **SURF-01**: `Threadline.OperatorSurface.Router` exposes a `threadline_operator_surface` macro.
- ✅ **SURF-02**: Phoenix/LiveView/HTML/PubSub declared `optional: true` in `mix.exs`.
- ✅ **SURF-03**: Modules gated via `Code.ensure_loaded?(Phoenix.LiveView)`. Zero overhead for capture-only adopters.
- ✅ **SURF-04**: `examples/threadline_phoenix` mounts the operator surface end-to-end behind a `phx.gen.auth`-style admin pipeline.

### UI — Operator screens
- ✅ **UI-01**: Incident drill-down LiveView at `/audit/transactions/:id` renders `Threadline.incident_bundle/2`.

- ✅ **UI-03**: Row history sub-view at `/audit/rows/:table/:pk` renders `Threadline.history/3` and `Threadline.as_of/4`.

### CLI — Mix-task parity (no-LiveView operator path)
