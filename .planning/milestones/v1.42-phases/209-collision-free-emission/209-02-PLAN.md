<objective>
Rewrite rerun detection so it identifies a table's earlier Threadline trigger migration by the canonical (schema, table) pair in the statement's ON clause, not by trigger name (roadmap SC2 support, NAME-03; D-08).

Purpose: name-based matching gave false positives (46-byte shared prefixes, public.a_b vs a.b) that make a later migration's rollback keep the wrong trigger, and false negatives on uncut pre-0.10 names. The pair is the identity 208 froze. This is pure generation-time text work, verified by unit tests, a property, and the Mix task.
Output: parse_triggers/1 + rerun?/2, the gen.triggers call site, frozen historical fixtures reused by later plans, and a shared naming-generator module.
</objective>
