<objective>
Close verification gap 2's follow-up. Planning prose that describes a machine-local path shape has tripped the guard twice. This plan makes the fix durable with a documented, contract-tested placeholder convention, and a guard hint that points to it when the guard fails. It also folds in WR-02 (colon filenames) and records a disposition for every review finding.

**Decision (the prose convention), rated reversible:** a placeholder convention, not an exemption. The user- or machine-specific segment is written as an angle-bracket placeholder (`<user>`, `<home>`, `<xx>`, `<path>`, `<project>`, `<claude-projects-dir>`). `<` is outside every family's segment character class, so placeholders never match, while the guard stays strict for everything else.

Rejected alternatives:
(1) Exempting `.planning/**/*-REVIEW.md` / `*-VERIFICATION.md`. These are exactly the files that quote real diffs and tool output, so an exemption would blind the guard where leaks are most likely. It also violates the "do not blanket-exempt `.planning/`" constraint.
(2) An inline suppression marker. That is an in-band escape hatch any real leak could carry.
(3) Widening the allowlist. It is forbidden for home paths by design.

Delivery is two-sided:
- The CONTRIBUTING section is the canonical rule, readable by contributors and by every GSD agent.
- The guard's stderr hint reaches the reviewer, verifier or executor at the exact moment it trips the gate. That is when the two prior incidents happened.
A doc contract test pins three things: the listed forms are clean, their concretized twins are HITs (non-vacuity), and the hint names the real heading.

**WR-02, folded (small and testable):** the matcher's `^([^:]*):([0-9]+):` parse cannot backtrack past a colon in a path. So a tracked file named with a colon is not just mis-scoped, as the review said: its lines are skipped entirely, a detection miss. The fix is SOH-delimited records from `git grep -z`, plus right-anchored parsing in bash.

**IN-01** was fixed in 217-06. **Deferred:** none.

Output: the convention section, the hint, colon-safe parsing, tests, and a fully dispositioned review.
</objective>
