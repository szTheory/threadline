## v1.46 — Operator UI Refinement

**Status:** Approved by the maintainer on 2026-10-08. Phases 238–243 are complete. Phase 243 passed verification with 16/16 must-haves on the exact candidate; its evidence index records the successful required CI run, candidate-bound preview, visual review, scoped recommendation, and remaining limitations.

**Delivery boundary:** Refine the eleven existing operator routes and their theme/export seams using the established Threadline identity and private Phoenix/HEEx primitives. Preserve host authentication, authorization, tenant scope, redaction, routes, public 1.x contracts and Phoenix optionality. Do not expand backend capabilities, introduce a framework or public component package, or revive paid visual judging.

**Starting condition:** Before visual defect classification or source changes, establish an isolated current-main implementation context through the configured workflow, carrying over the approved planning, PRODUCT.md and Impeccable context while preserving unrelated edits in the dirty milestone/v1.44 planning checkout. Record the actual source SHA, relevant diff and served-asset identity. The preflight's source candidates and historical images are inspection leads, not browser-reproduced defects.

**Ownership and regression:** Each approved requirement has one owning phase below. Responsive reflow and actual 200% zoom, Light/Dark/System appearance, keyboard/touch focus, truthful data and operation states, reduced-motion behavior, authorization and evidence fidelity remain acceptance conditions for every affected journey after their owning phase. Each journey closes with bounded rendered review, corrective work and confirmation; the final phase integrates that proof rather than deferring these concerns to a cleanup pass.

### Phase 239: Investigation entry and transaction composition

**Goal**: Operators can start, narrow and inspect an investigation in a readable, consistent composition that proves the shared UI approach.
**Depends on**: Phase 238
**Requirements**: UIS-01, UIS-02, UIS-03, UII-01, UII-02, UII-03, UIA-01, UIA-02, UIA-03, UIA-04
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
1. From home, operators can discover the supported lookup and search jobs, filter and page the timeline, and retain the active query and scope through reload and browser back/forward navigation.
2. Operators can inspect a transaction's changes and before/after evidence while clearly distinguishing its action, actor and execution context; abbreviated identifiers, values and timestamps have complete, authorized access paths with timezone information.
3. Operators can read the same canonical terms and a consistent hierarchy of labels, controls and evidence on home, timeline and transaction views, including dense and long-content states at narrow, intermediate and desktop widths and actual 200% browser zoom.
4. Operators can select Light, Dark or System and keep the chosen preference and effective appearance across reloads and LiveView navigation; keyboard and touch users can reach and dismiss controls and overlays with visible focus and predictable focus return, including overlapping UI states.
5. Operators can tell pending, empty, failed, denied and completed results apart without a stale result being relabeled as a new scope or an accepted operation being announced as complete.

### Phase 240: Connected investigation and recovery

**Goal**: Operators can follow related evidence and resume or share an investigation without losing its scope or trust in the controls.
**Depends on**: Phase 239
**Requirements**: UII-04, UII-05, UII-06, UIA-06, UIA-07
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
1. Operators can inspect supported row history and as-of evidence from direct entry and a transaction drawer, then return to the originating investigation with its context intact.
2. Operators can follow existing actor and correlation pivots to related activity and return without losing the active investigation context.
3. Operators can save, resume and share supported searches with coherent names and accurate restoration of supported search state.
4. Operators can copy authorized complete values on fresh direct entry and after repeated LiveView patches, with truthful failure feedback and no duplicate event handling.
5. Operators see purposeful, interruptible motion where state or spatial relationships change; frequent actions remain immediate and reduced-motion preferences are honored.
