    Section "Numeric legibility and reproduction constraints" (RES-03): testable constraints, not adjectives — every rule carries a number. Research and state: minimum stroke weight at 16px as a fraction of canvas (industry favicon guidance commonly lands near >=1/16 of canvas, i.e. >=1px at 16px — verify and cite); maximum count of distinguishable details/strokes at 16px; the silhouette-first test (mark must be identifiable from filled silhouette alone); minimum counter/gap size in px at 16px; the rule that favicons are designed at 16px and scaled up, not designed large and scaled down. Then monochrome/one-color reproduction rules: mark must survive a single flat color with no opacity tiers, no gradients, no overlaps-as-tone; name gradient-dependence as an explicit antipattern per CONTEXT.md (a mark whose identity disappears when the gradient is removed has no identity). Cite sources (favicon/iconography guidelines, type foundry or design-system documentation) with `Source:` lines. Each constraint must be phrased so Phase 161/162 can mechanically check it (e.g. "stroke >= 1.0px at 16px canvas" not "bold enough").
