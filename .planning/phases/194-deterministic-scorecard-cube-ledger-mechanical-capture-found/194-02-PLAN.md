<locked_capture_decisions>
Implements D-03 (capture matrix Tier A & evidence storage); resolves RESEARCH Claude's-Discretion / open items.

1. **Band 2 target pages: `transaction`, `coverage`, `retention`.** Rationale (recorded here): page scores are uniform 62 except home/timeline happy=72, so lowest-scoring cannot be read from scores — derive from design-debt signals instead. `transaction` and `coverage` each carry an explicit footgun entry (score 25: left-push centering; nested-card declutter); `retention` is the most structurally complex of the remaining pages (destructive flows, permission warnings). These maximize the chance mechanical checkers surface concrete violations.
2. **Tier A matrix:** Band 1 = all 11 pages × `happy` × {375, 768, 1280} × {dark, light} = 66. Band 2 = {transaction, coverage, retention} × {empty, error, permission-denied} × {375, 768, 1280} × {dark, light} = 54. Total 120 cells. ARIA YAML (`.aria.yml`) only for the deep band (Band 2).
3. **Cell-id** = `{ledger_id}__{theme}-{breakpoint}` (e.g. `page.transaction.empty__dark-1280`).
4. **Legacy 1024 Tier C baselines: KEEP at 1024.** They set 1024 inside the test and keep working regardless of the project viewport (RESEARCH Area 5); no rebaseline. The Tier C allowlist stays exactly 3 entries.
5. **Scorecard JSON is a RAW-inputs bundle, not pre-computed verdicts.** The browser writes: `cell_id`, `ledger_id`, `theme`, `breakpoint`, `captured_at`, `playwright_version`, resolved `tokens` (all `--tl-*`), `color_pairs` (per text/UI element: selector, color, backgroundColor, fontSize, fontWeight), `element_styles` (per element: selector, borderRadius, boxShadow, transitionDuration, fontSize + relevant margin/padding), `applied_colors` (chromatic colors used on non-background elements), `mode_b` structural counts (type_size_count, interactive_control_count, card_nesting_depth, scroll_cost, distinct-accent-hue *inputs* left to Elixir), `a11y_summary`, and `artifacts` paths. WCAG contrast, hue bucketing, and token-conformance verdicts are computed in Elixir (Plan 03) — the browser never runs the WCAG formula (keeps the gamma-2.4 formula single-sourced, Pitfall 4).
6. **Card selector set** (for `card_nesting_depth` DOM walk): `.tl-home__card, .tl-home__earned-panel, [class*="-panel"], [class*="-card"]` (confirm/adjust by grepping `style.ex`).
</locked_capture_decisions>
