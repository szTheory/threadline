<objective>
Harden the fix that plan 198-23 landed, at the three places REVIEW.md shows it is standing on nothing.

198-23 fixed a real collateral-deletion bug: `RetentionTail.run/1` called `Threadline.Retention.purge/1`
with no `:cutoff`, so the library's global-age default purged every organization's epoch-anchored demo
fiction rather than only `offboarded-co`'s intended backdate. The fix — an explicit, epoch-anchored
cutoff — is correct. What it rests on is not enforced:

- **WR-03** — the whole correctness of the fix depends on
  `@retention_purge_cutoff_days_before_epoch` being strictly less than `@org_y_backdate_days` and
  strictly greater than every other org's epoch offset. Nothing in code enforces either bound. Lower
  the backdate and the purge silently deletes nothing, failing later with a confusing "org Y not
  empty". Raise the cutoff and other orgs get swept. There is also a latent raise:
  `Retention.resolve_cutoff/2` raises `ArgumentError` when the requested cutoff is newer than the
  policy cutoff, and the requested cutoff is frozen while the policy cutoff is wall-clock derived.
- **WR-04** — the only documentation of why the cutoff value is safe cites an earliest other-org bound
  that is not the earliest. Two seeders anchor further back (`personas.ex:99`, `temporal.ex:37`). The
  conclusion still holds today, but a future reader tightening the cutoff toward the value the comment
  names would delete those setup rows.
- **WR-05** — the purge is still globally time-scoped, not org-scoped, so cross-org preservation is a
  coincidence and nothing asserts it directly; and `enable_retention!/0` permanently arms a `keep_days`
  policy in application env that any later `Retention.purge/1` call without an explicit cutoff will
  inherit, reproducing the exact bug 198-23 fixed.

Purpose: make the seed's safety properties enforced and legible rather than incidental and misdocumented.
Output: one hardened seeder module.
