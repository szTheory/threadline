<objective>
Close **CR-01**, the review's one finding that reaches into library source.

Round 4's stated fix was to eliminate a private duplicate that had drifted from the spec-tested
canonical `Threadline.OperatorSurface.Presentation.export_action_label/2`. What actually landed changed
one string literal. The private `defp` at `export_status_live.ex:476-493` still exists, is still the
only thing rendered at line 308, and `Presentation.export_action_label/2` is still called from nowhere
in `lib/` — only from `presentation_test.exs`. The divergence is live on three of five branches:

| readiness | `Presentation.export_action_label/2` | what actually renders |
|---|---|---|
| `:preparing` | `"Preparing download"` | `"Queued"` / `"Processing"` |
| `:needs_attention` | `"Reopen source search"` | `"Failed"` |
| `:unavailable` + expired | `"Export expired"` | `"Export expired"` |
| `:unavailable` + missing file | `"File unavailable"` | `"File unavailable"` |

The private copy also uses `to_string/1` rather than the canonical normalization path, and hardcodes
`DateTime.utc_now()` instead of honouring a `:now` option, so it cannot be tested at a frozen clock.

**Why this needs a decision and not just an edit.** The review's suggested fix — delete the private
function and render `Presentation.export_action_label/2` — is a **product-visible copy change** with a
measured blast radius: `"Queued"`, `"Processing"` and `"Failed"` are asserted in four e2e specs
(`operator-prove-mobile.spec.ts:57,109`, `operator-accessibility.spec.ts:608`,
`operator-screenshots.spec.ts:169-170`, `operator-features.spec.ts:146-147`) and are locked by the
Phase-186 contract test at `copy_contract_test.exs:443-463`, which explicitly names them as
"non-ready status text". There is also a real semantic question: `export_action_label/2` returns
*action* text ("Reopen source search"), while the rendered span is `role="status"`. Two defensible
directions exist, they differ in what a user sees, and the choice belongs to the maintainer.

Purpose: one owner for the export status copy contract, chosen deliberately, with every dependent
reconciled in the same change. Output: a decision record and a de-duplicated contract.
