<context>
**Two premises held during this session were falsified by measurement. Both are recorded
rather than quietly dropped, and a resuming agent should not re-derive them.**

1. **The rescope does NOT give `scroll_cost` per-page signal.** `show_ui_matrix?/1`
   (`stress_live.ex:794`) admits only `foundation primitive form_control group state`, so a
   `page.*` preview renders the header, a four-row `dl`, and one paragraph — **never the
   page under test**. Distinct values went 7 → 4, and after quantization all 120 page floors
   read exactly `0.5`. The plan's "first genuinely per-page MODE-B metric" claim was wrong,
   and the planned "floors are not all identical" assertion was **dropped rather than
   written to fail**. Giving the metric real signal needs `page.*` cells captured against
   real page content — out of scope.

2. **The footgun cell is a `status: "reserved"` placeholder.** Its PNG never contained a
   left-push layout, so the planned centering assertion would have invented coverage. The
   real `expectCenteredWithinColumn` invariant lives in `operator-phase-178-uat.spec.ts`
   (1024/1440) and is now pinned by a source scan.

**The governing insight, worth carrying forward:** the old document-wide read only *looked*
environment-stable because it measured a fixed sidebar catalog rather than the page. Scoping
it to real product content is what made it correct — and correctness is exactly what made it
sensitive to genuine cross-environment rendering differences. At 3-decimal precision it could
satisfy neither gate: CI's `0.654` would also have breached the `0.628` ratchet floor, so
excluding it from the byte-stability diff alone would have moved the failure to the next step
rather than fixing it.
</context>
