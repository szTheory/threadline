<objective>
Close the first of the three newly diagnosed red CI jobs: `Compile without optional deps`.

Purpose: `mix verify.compile_no_optional` (`mix.exs:121`, run at `ci.yml:155`) fails with
`module Phoenix.Component is not loaded and could not be found` and
`module Phoenix.Controller is not loaded`, ending in
`== Compilation error in file lib/threadline/operator_surface/ui.ex ==`. This is a real
library defect, not a test or CI-config problem: two `lib/` modules reference optional
Phoenix modules at compile time with no availability guard, while every one of their ~30
siblings has one. It is one of the eight red jobs holding `CI required` — and therefore
GREEN-07 and PR #26 — down.

Output: the two unguarded modules guarded in the established in-repo idiom, plus a derived-roster
contract test so the next unguarded module fails in its own diff rather than at the next CI run.

Measured survey (do not re-derive): of every file under `lib/` that references
`Phoenix.Component`, `Phoenix.LiveView`, `Phoenix.Controller`, or `Phoenix.HTML`, exactly two
lack a first-line `Code.ensure_loaded?` wrapper — `ui.ex` (1683 lines, `use Phoenix.Component`
at `:3`) and `controllers/theme_controller.ex` (91 lines, `use Phoenix.Controller` at `:2`).
A third file, `coverage/snapshot.ex`, matches the grep only inside a moduledoc that explicitly
states it is pure stdlib; it needs no change and must not get one.
</objective>
