<objective>
Close Gap 3: CI run 33183920952's `Tier A capture lane (byte-stable evidence)` fails its
`Assert byte-stable regeneration` step. `.planning/scorecards/page.coverage.error__light-1280.json`
regenerates with `"scroll_cost": 36.504` where the committed evidence says `19.038`.

Purpose: 198-10 made the bundle *count* assertion rot-proof but never touched byte-stability, so
this job stayed red for a cause nobody has yet diagnosed. `scroll_cost` on a `page.*` cell is a
function of rendered content height against the viewport, and a near-doubling is a large signal —
it is far more likely to be a real difference in what got rendered (data, fonts, layout, headless
flags, viewport) than floating-point noise.

Output: a written, measured diagnosis, and then — only if the diagnosis supports it — a fix that
removes the nondeterminism at its source.

**Do NOT make the assertion weaker to get green.** No tolerance band on the number, no path
exclusion for this one file, no `continue-on-error`, no conditional skip of the step. This phase
exists because of laundering, and a widened byte-stability gate is the purest available form of it.
If the honest answer turns out to be that the committed evidence is stale but regenerating it is
forbidden this milestone, that is a legitimate outcome to report — see Task 3's halt condition.
</objective>
