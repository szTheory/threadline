<objective>
Give **GREEN-07** and **roadmap success criterion 3** a terminal disposition for milestone v1.41.

**This is not a fix, and pretending otherwise is the failure mode.** Five gap-closure rounds have
converged on the same two red `needs:` members — `verify-capture` (Tier A scorecard byte-stability,
198 drifted files with `scroll_cost` values byte-identical across rounds 2 through 5) and
`verify-example-browser` (3 `operator-stress.spec.ts` ledger-baseline screenshot rows:
`page.home.happy`, `page.timeline.empty`, `footgun.transaction-page-left-push-desktop`). Both are red
**by construction** under standing maintainer decision **D-39**, which forbids their only mechanical
remedy — Tier-A `page.*` baseline regeneration — for the whole of this milestone. Round 5's verifier
routed this explicitly to human verification as a *milestone-level scope decision outside Phase 198's
own authority*.

So the honest output is a decision artifact plus its consequences, in the shape
`198-34-DECISION.md` already established for this phase. The executor presents the options, the
maintainer selects, the selection is recorded verbatim, and the selection propagates identically into
every record that mentions GREEN-07.

**Why a `checkpoint:decision` and not an executor judgment.** Recording GREEN-07 as accepted-Pending
for v1.41 is a **one-way door**: the milestone closes around that record, the roadmap's success
criterion is restated, and downstream phases (201, 203) are sized against a milestone whose CI
guarantee is now documented as knowingly unmet. That cannot be un-decided by a later edit without
rewriting the milestone's own history. Reversibility is rated `one-way` and the checkpoint blocks.

**What this plan explicitly may not do.** It may not regenerate a baseline (D-39). It may not narrow
`ci-required`'s `needs:`, touch `.github/`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `playwright.config.ts`,
`.planning/scorecards/` or any `*.png` (D-42) — the round-5 integrity audit verified a
`git diff --stat` over exactly that path set was empty, and round 6 must pass the same audit. It may
not mark GREEN-07 Complete, because no measured run has concluded `success` and D-01 admits nothing
else. Option (c) below authorizes a *future* scoped exception if the maintainer chooses it; it does
not execute one inside this plan.
