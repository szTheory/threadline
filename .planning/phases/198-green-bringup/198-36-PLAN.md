  SUMMARY and the tree disagree, record both statements side by side and mark the tree authoritative.
- **`deferred`** requires a reason and a named owner — a later plan, a later phase, or a
  milestone-level constraint — plus a matching appended entry in `deferred-items.md` written in
  Task 2.
- **`no-action-required`** requires the reason. IN-04 is the clear case: it is REVIEW.md's own list of
  verified-correct changes and asks for nothing. Record it as such rather than leaving it out.

  outcome and must be stated; omitting the row is not.
