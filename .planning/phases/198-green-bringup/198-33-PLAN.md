<objective>
Close the two review Criticals and two Warnings that live in the Phase-177 and Phase-135 UAT specs —
the places where round 4's rewrites traded away coverage the suites were carrying.

- **CR-04** — `operator-phase-177-uat.spec.ts` replaced a hard-coded 12-element `groupStories` array
  with a runtime lookup whose only cardinality assertion is `expect(ids.length).toBeGreaterThan(0)`.
  `stress_fixtures.ex:72` declares exactly 12. If a refactor drops eleven of them, or the story list
  is truncated or paginated, or `.tl-stress__story-id` is renamed such that only a subset resolves,
  the suite reports green on one twelfth of its prior coverage.
- **WR-07** — the same helper never asserts the returned ids are group stories.
  `stress_live.ex:56` resolves the category filter via `allow(params["category"], @category_allowlist)`;
  an unrecognised value yields `nil` and `filter_by(:category, nil)` applies **no filter at all**, so a
  renamed category silently returns the entire catalog under a group-coverage banner.
- **WR-06** — twelve independent tests, each with its own 120s budget for five navigations, were
  collapsed into one test performing roughly 61 navigations, 60 viewport reflows and 120 assertions
  inside a single 120s budget. `test.step` failures abort the enclosing test, so a failure on story 1
  leaves stories 2-12 unproven but unreported, and a CI retry re-runs all 60 navigations to retry one.
- **CR-05** — `operator-phase-135-uat.spec.ts`'s test named *"support user is denied admin-only
  Coverage"* now asserts `Unsupported.descriptor(:coverage_unavailable)`, whose own body text reads
  *"Coverage is unavailable in this support lane. This is not a permissions issue."* The descriptor is
  a lane-capability signal, not a role signal. The test passes in a world where authorization works
  **and** in a world where Coverage is unavailable to every role including admin, and there is no
  admin-sees-Coverage counterpart anywhere in the file.

The round-4 goal of rot-resistance was legitimate and is **kept**. What is restored is what was
separable from it and got dropped anyway: the floor, the identities, the filter proof, the per-story
verdicts, and the discriminating half of an authorization test.

Purpose: stop two suites from reporting coverage they do not measure. Output: two hardened UAT specs.
