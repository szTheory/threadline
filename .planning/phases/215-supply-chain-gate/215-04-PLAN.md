<objective>
Tell the maintainer about new advisories and stale dependencies without a pull-request flood
(SUP-04). This plan adds a weekly, non-required `deps-health.yml` lane that upserts one `ci-deps`
issue, and a documented freshness policy in CONTRIBUTING that a doc-contract test holds to the
workflow. The policy section also documents plan 03's `hex_audit_ignores/0` convention, which
closes SUP-03's contributor-facing half.

Design choices (Claude's discretion):
- The lane covers all three lockfiles, per the orchestrator's option (b) and RESEARCH A4.
- The logic is extracted into `bin/deps-health-report`, following the `bin/classify-flake-run`
  precedent, so the classification is unit-tested rather than living as untested inline workflow
  shell.
- `outdated` alone is informational: the issue is filed and the job stays green. `advisory` and
  `unknown` turn the job red.
- Hex `cooldown` is not adopted, per the orchestrator's option (d). The policy says so in one line.

Output: script, workflow, CONTRIBUTING section, two test files.
</objective>
