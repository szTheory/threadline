<objective>
Remove the capture/semantics Ecto associations (API-07) while every reader of `transaction.action` keeps the same shape.

Purpose: The capture layer (`AuditTransaction`) must stop declaring an association to the semantics layer (`AuditAction`) and vice versa (CLAUDE.md three-layer rule; D-05, D-06). The exploration layer takes over the join through one hidden batched hydrate helper (D-08, D-09). The public `:preload` paths that named `:action` keep working with one deprecation warning (D-10), so the 1.0 API-freeze release has no hard break here.

Output: `Threadline.Query.hydrate_actions/3`, association-free schemas, rewired internal call sites, the deprecation shim, two new test files, two updated source-literal tests, and a CHANGELOG entry (D-07).
</objective>
