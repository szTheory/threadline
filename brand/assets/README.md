## Fonts

- `fonts/web/fonts.css`
- `fonts/variable/FamiljenGrotesk[wght].ttf`
- `fonts/variable/Geist[wght].ttf`
- `fonts/static/IBMPlexMono-Regular.ttf`
- `fonts/OFL-FamiljenGrotesk.txt`

All logo SVGs are flat filled paths (no live text, no strokes, no masks) in exact brand colors, transparent. `light` files are for light backgrounds, `dark` for dark ones; `mono-ink` and `mono-fog` are one-color drawings, not recolors. `icon/favicon.svg` swaps its ink with the device theme; the ICO is light ink only. Fonts are SIL Open Font License 1.1 (licenses in `fonts/`). This edition has no logo animation and no sound files.
